How to play

Goal

Your car races forward on its own. Swipe left or right on the road (or tap the left/right side of it) to change lanes, or use the arrow keys / A and D. Dodge the rival traffic coming down the other two lanes — getting boxed in costs a life.

Traffic

Other chains' vehicles drift down the road, each labelled with its own ticker, purely for flavour — they all behave the same. Switch lanes early; the road gets busier and faster the longer you last.

XNO nitro

A glowing XNO coin drifts down a lane now and then — drive into it for a nitro burst: a few seconds of invincibility that lets you plough straight through traffic, plus double score while it lasts.

Scoring

You score for distance covered, plus a bonus for every vehicle you dodge — the bonus grows with your combo. Getting hit, or letting a vehicle you were boxed in by, resets the combo.

Modes

Daily: the same traffic for everyone, new at 00:00 UTC — dodge 60 vehicles to clear the day. Quick 60: 60 seconds, score as much as you can. Survival: endless, traffic gets faster and busier — how long can you last?

Lives and ranks

You start with 3 lives (4 for supporters), with a short shield after each hit. Total vehicles dodged earns a rank: Newbie, Hodler (150), Diamond Hands (500), Whale (1,500) and Satoshi (4,000).

Supporters

A tip of 0.01 XNO or more unlocks the extra accent colours and a 4th life. Tips are optional and the whole game is free.
